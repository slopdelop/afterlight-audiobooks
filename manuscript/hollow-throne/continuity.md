# Canon audit for the completion of The Hollow Throne

## Scope and authority

Read in full: the canonical Afterlight reader JSON for chapters 6–16, eleven chapters totaling approximately 43,500 words, and the user's completion handoff. The chapter prose, rather than earlier audio-oriented `chapter.txt` copies, is the source for normal spellings. No established chapter was edited.

Chapters 1–5 are not present in the project's chapter output directories or the published reader catalog. A narrow search of the user's attachment directory for their supplied chapter names did not locate the missing prose. Their handoff summaries are therefore provisional canon; do not manufacture replacement chapters or claim to have read them. The original user also said they listened to the first five elsewhere.

The supplied completion plan calls for chapters 17–31 and a hopeful, decisive ending. It permits improvements to the route, but prohibits rewriting chapters 1–16, resurrection of Palpatine, reversal of Anakin's redemption, instant universal conditioning, and sequel-bait.

## What the heroes know, and what only the reader knows

The reader has seen Palpatine's private conduct and the children's early experiments. The protagonists have not witnessed these scenes; learning their content needs a witness, a recoverable document, or corroborated testimony. They must not know private Sith thoughts automatically.

The heroes know:

- Five stations: Mos Eisley, Bespin, Dagobah, second Death Star-associated command systems, Coruscant palace.
- Aurel Seven refers to the Alderaanian prisoner program. Forty-three living people were recently moved; Darren is the separately tracked Subject/Voice Profile 044.
- Voices and even entire convincing video conversations can be fabricated. Valid paperwork is also being abused.
- Ordinary copied voices deceive but cannot physically compel an unprepared listener.
- Conditioning requires prepared subjects, repeated exposure, and specific triggers; resistance, retained awareness and damage are documented.
- The Meridian is a physical Imperial transport in an old logistics consortium originating in Damask Holdings and absorbed into Palpatine's estate. Veyr was added as authorized executor six days before the earlier investigation.
- The route is segmented: a transfer station knows its next hop; the complete journey is stored elsewhere. The heroes identified the ship by a matching departure window, passenger capacity, declared medical cargo, repeated suspicious journeys, and command-key records, not by already decrypting its final destination.
- Sim Aloo's credentials contain a valid private signature derived from an original authentication device. They are not merely a familiar recorded voice or a forgeable name.
- Witness Zero has a structured nonordinary-audio pattern; different listeners hear different familiar voices. They have not established a controlling person inside it.

The reader additionally knows:

- Plagueis deliberately preserved instructions after death; his household droid retained records and packed the ceramic spindle separately, with a concealed blue indicator under its hand.
- Palpatine destroyed many private training/voice files but kept other useful research; his effort was incomplete.
- Palpatine repeatedly forbade reconstruction of his own voice, command credentials, and autonomous ordering systems. He has not yet issued his final comprehensive shutdown in available prose.
- The young Bespin researcher at the end of chapter 13 heard his dead mother's voice and received instructions to tell Veyr rather than the Emperor.
- Sela and Iven survived the Temple attack and early experiments; all five children were alive when transferred. One was medically unstable, but that child's identity and subsequent fate are not yet specified.
- Veyr privately saved their names and wrote that interpersonal attachments might be a mechanism for reversing conditioning. He was already capable of accurately understanding this and electing to conceal it.

## Chapters 6–16: exact continuity anchors

### Chapter 6

Han and Chewbacca land in bay 94, enter Wuher's cantina and open its hidden station. The installation is old, with some Clone Wars-era equipment and recent replacements. The famous Luke/Obi-Wan encounter was identified retrospectively after Yavin, not watched in advance as an omniscient Imperial trap. The station contains surveillance and supply records, not evidence the forty-three prisoners physically lived beneath this cantina.

Voice Profile 044 resembles Darren. Its original sample was acquired in Imperial year fourteen; authorized use is witness conditioning; a quoted reconstruction quality is 98.7 percent. Sim Aloo's authorization sent an emergency activation toward Coruscant on the morning Darren jumped. The heroes copied the data and escaped a security purge. Han's ending threat came from an unidentified transmitter in Mos Eisley.

### Chapter 7

Immediately after Yavin, Palpatine learns Luke's name and infers his ancestry. He withholds it from Vader. This is established fan-novel canon and must not be replaced with outside-continuity timelines.

Crucially, Darren is described as an Alderaanian and **one of the prisoners held under Aurel Seven**. Palpatine orders him kept alive and transferred into advanced conditioning. Thus the handoff's distinction between Darren and the forty-three must mean different people/current inventories, not that Darren was never associated with Aurel Seven. Safest resolution: an original numbered forty-fourth subject was removed from the captive ledger into palace service, leaving forty-three in transport custody.

### Chapter 8

Bespin lab property was leased to an engineering consortium fourteen years before the present and passed through companies into an Imperial research authority. It was officially decommissioned shortly before Imperial occupation, but still drew power until around Endor. Distinguish underlying older facilities, legal lease, and Veyr's period of management rather than claiming the entire installation was built for Han.

Secret monitoring began seventeen seconds before Han entered carbon freezing; Leia was observed too. Vader had his own purpose, and Veyr exploited it. A note recommends using the findings on Aurel Seven. Lando did not authorize these experiments and is genuinely distressed; his past betrayal must not become permanent disloyalty. Leia used a small, uncertain Force-assisted effort to move a pressure lever. Her abilities may grow, but she is not already a trained Jedi.

### Chapter 9

Dagobah Imperial trials ran over the previous eleven years. The carved chamber is far older, built around the dark-side nexus, with a shaft approximately twenty meters deep. Machines measured the cave without fully recording its visions. Luke's imagined older tyrant is a possible temptation, never a destined future. Luke explicitly recognizes that the Vader apparition is not his redeemed father.

Reconstructed Yoda came from fragments of Senate, Temple and Clone Wars records, not discovery of Yoda's exile address. Luke identifies the fake through its content and lack of authentic teaching. R2 recovered most files, and Luke rescued him when the chamber flooded. A separate protected ancient recording/control device survived and said “Begin the next sequence.” This dangling process needs an explanation or shutdown payoff; it need not be a new sentient enemy.

### Chapter 10

Plagueis died thirty-two years before Yavin, on the eve of Palpatine's election. The household droid and spindle predate the Empire. The spindle came from the chamber entrance on Dagobah, and Plagueis admitted its composition had no unusual properties. Do not suddenly make the ceramic spindle an all-powerful Force battery: it can be a passive index, alignment shape, preserved reference, or physically significant artifact whose context matters.

Plagueis's initial woman died forty-seven years before the demonstration. Her widower supplied memories, but did not recognize the garden reference. Witness Zero therefore cannot be explained solely as that particular woman's literal garden. The droid preserves a further standing instruction not completed on the page. Its long service allows retained delegated tasks and records, not unlimited new motivations or replacement-villain status.

### Chapter 11

Han rescues medical vessel Starling, Captain Ressa Vale, fourteen passengers plus crew/cargo. The attacking customs interceptors and buoy carry fraudulent orders with real executive credentials. Their purpose demonstrates how ordinary people can be trapped administratively without psychic control. Captured buoy becomes the heroes' original physical command key.

One hostile vessel calls itself Aurek Seven. That ship name is not the prisoner-program spelling Aurel Seven. Preserve the distinction if referenced.

The decoded current transfer carries forty-three people and Veyr's receiving authority, two days before Han's journey. The original Darren recording ends “I want to go home.” Alderaan's destruction was concealed or manipulated in his conditioning. Do not assume all survivors already know all news, or that none does.

### Chapter 12

Venn isolates the captured key, blocks network contact, and uses its authorization to open restricted records. Han's old biometric information bypasses a demand for an original observation subject; Han was not newly exposed to conditioning. The archive labels Leia a primary observation subject, whereas the earlier monitoring file called her secondary. These are different study stages/records, not a reason to call either scene fraudulent.

The garden file produces different voices for Leia (Bail), Han (Leia), Lando (himself), while a conventional analyzer shows static. The Aurel cohort reinforces memories with stories, jokes, songs, names and places. No single object or phrase is a universal cure.

The end contains an apparently impossible communication on Venn's isolated terminal thanking her for locating the Meridian. Ground it in the copied authorization module's local stored conditional payload or an embedded device channel explicitly found in the hardware; do not quietly grant internet access to air-gapped systems.

### Chapter 13

At Hoth/Bespin time, Veyr says he has maintained the Bespin facility approximately eleven years. The legal lease in chapter 8 says fourteen years before the current date roughly one year later. Those dates need not match if the research installation predates its later front-company lease or if “maintained” includes predecessor premises. Don't add unsupported arithmetic that makes a minor discrepancy larger.

Palpatine forbids direct conditioning of Leia and says observe only. Ordinary freezing, public observation and trust-voice exposure do not retroactively prepare Luke, Leia, Han or Lando for motor compulsion.

Nearby prisoners unexpectedly resist when Leia reacts to Han's loss. One man says “I remember the garden.” The account presents correlation, not a proven remote love-powered cure. Later Luke should understand and help create conditions for resistance; there is no need to establish a reproducible mass psychic liberation broadcast.

The researcher at the ending heard a voice in static and saw local messages telling him to inform Veyr. Plagueis is not thereby alive. Palpatine's vision of the green saber and Vader was incomplete; he did not foresee or consent to his defeat.

### Chapter 14

Luke distinguishes an imitation of Obi-Wan from a spirit by the absence of Obi-Wan's presence. Luke hears extra personalized words; the others hear different reassuring speech. The carrier is a recurring structured energy pattern, not conventional speech in the file.

Yoda's genuine spirit says the cave is older than the Republic, its darkness predates the carved enclosure, and he used it partly to hide his presence. He regrets not understanding/stopping the Imperial work. He heard the garden phrase before the Empire, and does not know its full meaning. The garden phenomenon must not be reduced entirely to Veyr's invention or Sela's toy.

Yoda teaches the distinction among memory, vision and command, and assistance rather than choosing on another's behalf. Do not undo that through a Force command that makes every subject become free on Luke's say-so.

The records trace a packet from Dagobah via relays to the Meridian, marked PRIMARY WITNESS STATUS UNCONFIRMED / ACTIVATE SECONDARY RETRIEVAL PROTOCOL, twenty-three minutes before Darren jumped. The same relay delivered the Mos Eisley command. There is also a current post-flood process and reply saying “The subject remembers. Continue.” Separate original message creation, stored retransmission, present replay, and reception timestamps explicitly to resolve the loose chronology.

### Chapter 15

Darren's fall was from a palace window more than a kilometer above the lower city; an abandoned diplomatic pad four hundred meters below the office could intercept him. Work order three days before fall; manual bypass and emergency power. A helper rode down alone and left with him. Medical readings forty minutes after the fall: multiple fractures, internal bruising, mild concussion, significant blood loss. Preserve a limp, fatigue, healing limitations and the need for assistance in the next few days; he should not immediately run a whole assault like an uninjured soldier.

The hidden care room used municipal stock, had no named patient, and was later compromised into a voice-camera/security-droid trap. The helper's move to another safe location must be deliberate. The helper showed two fingers to the camera, an old Alderaanian resistance received/safe-route signal Bail had taught Leia; this can intersect with the children's two/three-tap practice without pretending the earlier meanings were identical.

Hard timing seam: the prose says field switched on fourteen seconds before the jump but stayed active four seconds. Literal interpretation makes rescue impossible. Resolve once in new prose: fourteen-second marker is arming/priming rather than the later four-second load-bearing interception pulse, or independently prove the two log clocks differ. Do not rewrite the published chapter or repeat the impossible calculation.

### Chapter 16

Begins seven weeks after Order 66 and then spans approximately a month of experiments. Sela is approximately ten; Iven thirteen. In the present about twenty-three years later, they should be approximately thirty-three and thirty-six, not children or elderly survivors. Veyr was about thirty, hence now around fifty-three. He joined Damask work seven years before that first Imperial audience, after Plagueis died; he never met Plagueis. He cannot later reminisce about working personally with his Sith master.

Five children are transferred alive after the month; one is medically unstable, unnamed. Three of the five remain unnamed. They are not members of the forty-three-person Alderaanian transport cohort by implication. Their destination has black stone, is colder, eleven hours by that transport from Coruscant, but no planet is named.

Sela's wooden star came from Master Varo and was confiscated; the last prose establishes it in a storage cabinet. If restored physically, show credible custody, conservation or recovery rather than magically handing it to her. The shared teacher's voice was used on Iven. Two taps ask “Are you there?”, three answer “I'm here”; a longer unspecified sequence means “Remember.”

Minor transcript seam: Sela whispers “Iven” while Veyr says Iven was reminding her of her own name. It is safe to treat this as reciprocal naming/remembering each other, without dwelling on the lab report's imperfect description.

The ending mentions “a new Custodian” despite naming Veyr throughout. Prefer a change in office, powers or self-altered identity, not an unplanted second Veyr or extra mastermind.

## Rules the conclusion must establish and obey

1. **Forgery:** convincing recorded audio/video plus legitimate inherited authorization can deceive people and old systems. Proof of command validity is not proof of speaker consent.
2. **Conditioning:** substantial prior preparation, selected subjects, trigger-specific responses and reinforcement. Retained internal refusal is possible while muscles or speech obey. Mental/neurological damage is a cost, not evidence that victimhood is weakness.
3. **Witness Zero:** a preserved stimulus/Force-associated reflection recruits a listener's own memories; conventional audio may contain only static. It is distinct from normal synthesized voices and distinct from autonomous authentic Force spirits.
4. **Primary Witness:** not yet mechanically defined. New design should anchor identities/baselines and command associations in a unique material reference, with sharply bounded reading/writing/selection functions. Breaking command transmission does not itself heal long conditioning. Lost personal records cannot be conjured out of nothing.
5. **Authorization:** Sim's live-use credentials require a diverted original signing device or delegated signing capability, not resurrection. Specify what revokes it and why ordinary retired Imperial infrastructure still trusts it.
6. **Distributed system:** all five mapped sites need a distinct useful role or recovered contribution. A central stop must require physical access, credential handling, communications disruption and evacuation, not one universal password.
7. **Force:** Luke can perceive, defend, save, and offer contact; he cannot impose recovery or make someone else's moral choice. The cave reflects possible futures, not destiny. Redeemed Anakin remains real; Palpatine remains dead.
8. **Recovery:** removing imposed authority gives opportunities for action but does not restore every memory, injury, skill or relationship immediately. The ending should provide care and self-determined futures.

## Writing guidance from the full reread

- The remaining story needs choices changing events, rather than five more labs proving the same principle. Use a rescue, contested object, physical deadline, compromised route, or failed trust decision in every major sequence.
- Do not turn briefings into page after page of a novice question, Veyr/Venn explanation, “And?”, “Why?”, silence, and another screen entry. Let readers infer from meaningful actions and consequences.
- Anonymous terminal messages end chapters 6, 12, 13 and 15, while ancient devices close 9 and 14. Retire this ending pattern. Finish on departures, choices, altered relationships, physical reversals or earned stillness.
- Avoid adding a new larger mystery every time an old clue is explained. New chapters should close the existing inventory.
- Han's jokes are best when pressure or affection motivates them. Allow sustained competence, anger and listening. He must not joke over every prisoner's pain.
- Leia's distinctive perspective should involve leadership decisions and Alderaanian knowledge, not only being the person who asks what a report means.
- Lando should own difficult logistics and leadership decisions. Chewbacca should originate solutions rather than only growl and perform demolition. C-3PO's language/protocol expertise must matter; R2's access should have time, risk and physical limits.
- Keep close viewpoint and sensory geography during action. Existing prose jumps from named feelings to explanatory summaries; let bodies, spaces, pauses and decisions carry emotion.
- Do not stack fragments and “Not X. Not Y. But Z.” constructions through every revelation. Flowing conventional paragraphs will improve audiobook rhythm.
- State each technical rule clearly in a consequential scene, then trust it. Avoid reiterating the theme after a concrete image already delivered it.
- Preserve warmth and earned hope. The children and prisoners can be damaged without being narratively erased. Defeating control requires granting choices that are inconvenient to the heroes as well as choices the heroes like.
