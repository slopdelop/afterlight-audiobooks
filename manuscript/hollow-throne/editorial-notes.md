- Use conventional paragraphs, an intelligible viewpoint within each scene, concrete sensory details and clear action geography. Scene breaks may use `***`.
- Every chapter needs a change in circumstances, a decision with consequences or an earned answer. Information has to enable action; do not build another chain of passive briefings.
- Give Han, Leia, Luke, Lando, Chewbacca and both droids distinct useful contributions. Humor should grow from the situation and personality, not interrupt every serious exchange.

- Preserve hope: Palpatine is dead, Anakin's redemption stands, Han and Leia stay together, and the Republic and future Jedi have worthwhile futures.
