# Ending architecture proposal — chapters 17–31

This is planning, not manuscript. Existing chapters remain untouched. Source: complete handoff plus complete canonical reader text of chapters 14–16. Earlier events are provisionally from the handoff and the canon auditor's findings; manuscript takes precedence.

## Nonnegotiable rules and reconciliations

- Palpatine and Sim Aloo died at Endor. Anakin's redemption stands. No surviving Sith soul, hidden mastermind, malicious all-powerful AI, or revived Emperor.
- Physical commands require an individually enrolled, repeatedly conditioned subject, prepared associations, and a usable delivery path. Unconditioned people can be frightened, fooled, or persuaded, not automatically compelled. Luke and Leia's trials in chapter 28 are emotionally persuasive deceptions, never retroactive secret enrollment.
- Apparent voices in Witness Zero are perceptual experiences elicited by a Force-associated pattern; ordinary archive speakers can also reproduce actual waveform-based cloned voices. Keep the two mechanisms distinct. The pattern can be dangerous to the unconditioned without making it an obedience switch.
- Chapter 14 explicitly establishes the garden phrase predates Plagueis. It cannot originate in Sela's childhood, Bail's garden, or an Imperial code phrase. It names a recurring remembered refuge in an ancient nexus; its precise first speaker remains unknown. Different people experience the same offered recollection through their own associations. Its human meaning is ownership of remembered life, not an encoded universal password.
- The ceramic spindle is a passive resonant focus taken from the cave, not a portable soul. Plagueis's household droid preserved it and a chain of research custody under standing instructions. The droid's actions and routine automated telemetry account for the lingering machinery; Veyr's remote supervision accounts for purposeful modern instructions. Neither is an additional antagonist.
- The black prism is the Primary Witness: a unique physical reference/authority core built by the Damask laboratories from resonant materials, subsequently developed by Veyr. Two logical functions share it: read-only identity baselines and a writable association/authority index. Its loss blocks reliable mass recalibration, not every cached command. Raw copies of voices, and limited cached instructions, already exist elsewhere.
- Baselines are records of interviews, observed responses, remembered associations, and measurements. They are tools for patient-led rehabilitation, not bottled consciousness. Retaining them helps patients; deleting them would strand useful recovery evidence. Do not make the ethical choice a false claim that all recovery becomes impossible without the prism.
- Five stations are a distributed engineering chain, not five magical batteries that must stay powered: Mos Eisley vocabulary/traffic collection and relay history; Bespin enrollment/baselines and adaptive experiments; Dagobah resonance calibration; Endor master authorization/notary copy; Palace command distribution and reference synchronization. Old measurements can be cached. Finding their roles leads to concrete disconnection points.
- Chapter 7 places Darren among Aurel Seven. Reconcile by making him its original forty-fourth admission and later the separately stationed command-interface test subject. The other forty-three remain the surviving prisoner group. The five former Temple children are an earlier, distinct cohort.
- Chapter 16's five children, age ten/thirteen at Order 66, make Sela about thirty-three and Iven about thirty-six now. They are adults. The narrator's phrase 'new Custodian' can mean the office's expanded remit/its later form; do not introduce a secret second Veyr to patch it.
- Chapter 14 contains a chronology contradiction in the flooding/transmission explanation. Future dialogue distinguishes the older queued retrieval packet's original time from later post-flood relay acknowledgments/retransmission. The twenty-three-minute lead before Darren's jump remains the authoritative event. Do not claim Luke was on Dagobah before his already-written visit.

## Fixed human payoffs

### Darren and the undercity

Proposed rescuer: adult Sela, now working under a municipal medic/maintenance identity. She escaped years earlier and joined a modest Alderaanian courier relief cell. The two-finger camera gesture is that cell's established safe-route sign, distinct from her childhood two-knock/three-knock communication. She recognized the old program in Darren because she had helped with clandestine treatment and refugee routes; she is not omniscient or a Jedi master in hiding.

Three days before the fall, Darren learned from the next scheduled credential audit that the prism's retrieval contingency could be invoked. Unable safely to confess, he preserved the Aurel ledger where an incoming Republic review would encounter it, submitted the platform work order using his own authority, and contacted Sela through the old relief route. The order that forced his actions was to take the Primary Witness, clear obstacles, and reach the designated extraction route. Fighting the guards/opening the cabinet were imposed; refusing the route that would deliver him back to Veyr, and jumping toward the only escape he had prepared, were his own desperate variation. Sela's manual observation of the tower's emergency state and a prearranged short signal gave her the fourteen-second start. The field's four-second logged energy spike is the full-load interception interval, not a contradiction with standby being armed fourteen seconds before the jump. Darren suffered genuine fractures and ongoing weakness; he does not become an action hero without limits.

He marked the forty-three as living at considerable personal risk. His wish to go home is allowed to evolve: Alderaan is gone, and he chooses a future without becoming responsible for everyone's rescue or forgiving his abusers.

### Sela and Iven

Sela escaped six years before the present with one other survivor, proposed Nemi, who now lives under a protected civilian identity and can be contacted after the climax. Two of the original five, proposed Mira and Oren, died in separate earlier captivity/transfer incidents; recover names and definite fates, do not use missing names as sequel bait. Iven remained in custody, now a technically skilled caretaker of other prisoners because the staff exploited his capacity to stabilize them. He is aboard the Meridian as an unmanifested old-program subject, distinct from the forty-three Alderaanians. Rescue count is explicitly forty-three Alderaanians plus Iven and any consenting innocent crew. His and Sela's reunion uses the knock pattern quietly, not as a magical cure. She recovers the confiscated wooden star in evidence after the network falls. Luke offers assistance, never automatically enrolls them in a school or declares trauma makes them suitable teachers.

### Veyr

His private childhood fear of arbitrary authorities is optional; do not introduce a lengthy tragic backstory. What matters is his observed choice to treat moral hesitation as a defect. He conditioned himself over years to suppress doubt and remorse, then installed escalating reinforcement when his results remained imperfect. Show signs before chapter 29: pauses that vanish after a hand-to-neck gesture or timed pulse, rigid repetition of an optimization despite new human evidence, a deleted warning acknowledged without any behavioral change. He remains accountable: he chose the procedure and its renewal. No one-line appeal restores a good man. Luke disables immediate threats, protects conditioned guards, captures Veyr, and ensures he receives treatment and trial.

## Chapter sequence and boundary states

### 17

Start after chapter 15: Leia/Han/Chewbacca/Threepio and Orl on Coruscant; Luke with Ackbar arranging Endor expedition; Lando/Venn on Bespin. Republic contractors begin stripping a supposedly decommissioned palace relay, inadvertently triggering an authenticated Imperial shutdown procedure. Leia must evacuate technicians and save a living maintenance officer who has been assigned to burn evidence, rather than watch a recording. A physical interlock produces an authentic limited destruction warrant in Palpatine's own old signing chain. Threepio distinguishes 'return references for destruction' from 'delete local copies' before the wipe completes. They save enough serial provenance to find an ejectable Endor notary unit; lose an easy local route to the command roots. Close with an intentional expedition decision. Orl and Leia continue safe human inquiries; avoid claiming Darren's already-known survival as revelation.

### 18

Han, Chewbacca, Luke, and Artoo work with Ackbar-authorized salvage around Endor. A hardened command/notary module was ejected before the station breakup and caught in a debris cluster; no intact throne room. Radiation, rotating wreckage, rival Imperial salvagers and limited tether time create one coherent set piece. Luke rescues an enemy salvager instead of pursuing a clean shot. Chewbacca repairs a jammed grapple and takes a physical risk; Han flies by hand through uncertain debris; Artoo recovers a bounded component while losing access to a larger archive. Obtain a paired-seal discrepancy proving a valid authorization device left the Emperor's custody before Endor, plus a restricted routing address tied to Veyr's current logistics. One rescued salvage worker's testimony has to be protected. Leia's team holds its undercity contact open rather than abandoning it to join everyone at Endor.

### 19 — historical

Set shortly before the already-written chapter 4 departure, or aboard the travel route before its decisive wafer count, respecting auditor's exact chronology. Sim diverts a valid issued wafer during a transfer/recertification and substitutes a receipt/blank whose count remains formally correct. Veyr supplies the technical deception; Greejatus seeks leverage and self-preservation rather than matching ideology. They copy permissions, not magical new Palpatine biometrics. Sim wants a post-Endor bargaining asset, expects Palpatine to win, and does not plan his own death. Palpatine catches a lesser instance of one of his old instructions being invoked out of context. His threat is specific and genuinely frightening. End with the real wafer physically placed in a courier module and its successor destination established.

### 20

Present; reunion at Coruscant after credible travel time. A live attempt to revoke captured credentials exposes a dependency: mass revocation would also trip the captive-care unit's failsafe lock. Han refuses to gamble prisoners on an untested shutdown. Lando and Venn bring Bespin enrollment metadata and demonstrate a bounded test with a harmless legacy machine, not a human. Threepio maps scope and revocation grammar; Artoo verifies actual sender times versus queued acknowledgments, cleaning up the chapter 14 mismatch. Establish station chain in the act of saving a civilian relay, tracing the Meridian's licensed maintenance support to a concealed near-Coruscant logistics berth. The missing piece is a living subject's baseline reference and safe deconditioning procedure—Darren—not another encrypted file. Sela offers a specific meeting through the contact Leia has been cultivating.

### 21

Leia and a minimal party reach Darren via a relief clinic during a pressure raid or targeted municipal sweep; he mistrusts uniforms and demands that Leia put away her transmitter. Earn trust by following that request despite operational inconvenience. Sela helps others leave before her own evacuation. Darren controls the telling and can stop. Explain ledger, escape preparation, capture/Aurel numbering, prism, and wounds through urgent choices/interrupted practical action, not an uninterrupted monologue. He will lend the prism for a carefully bounded read, refuses total exposure, and chooses to help identify the prisoners. End: Darren and Sela protected in an independent civilian clinic outside palace systems; core team knows Sela's identity, and she learns Iven may be on the ship. Prism in a shielded physical case, custody explicitly assigned to Darren/Leia. Sela does not join the old Alderaanian forty-three cohort.

### 22 — final historical chapter

Hours before Endor. Palpatine authorizes immediate local cessation and final destruction on his personal confirmation after victory. He withholds the final irreversible key because no subordinate may erase a source of his power independently. Veyr and Sim have shifted the root's maintenance authority to the diverted wafer; Veyr files false cessation acknowledgments while preserving reference systems under prisoner medical continuity. Sim's wafer is already off-station. Palpatine's suspicions make him cut ordinary escape traffic, which kills the advisers' hope of personally fleeing but does not recall the earlier courier. His arrogance is choosing exclusive control over safe automatic termination. End before Luke enters the climactic movie confrontation. No new visions of surviving it.

### 23

In independent clinic/workshop. Darren consents to one low-output reference comparison, with a physical disconnect in his own reach and Sela monitoring. A cached prompt in his own voice produces a painful partial motor response; he uses a chosen external cue to reach the disconnect, proving both danger and remaining agency. No manufactured trauma as a heroic exam: he devised the test and may end it. Baseline reads allow identification and choice of anchors, not instant cure. Threepio discovers the shutdown clause cancels imposed authority bindings while preserving evidence if each local care channel is separately isolated first. Team constructs rescue and rollback with a tabletop/empty-device test and real evacuation resources. Plan the Meridian boarding through a licensed maintenance window. End: teams depart with separate jobs; prism never connected to palace wireless by accident.

### 24

Meridian in a concealed industrial transfer berth near Coruscant (or close orbit with explicit flight time). It is both transport and mobile enrollment/maintenance clinic, explaining old retrieval packets. Boarding requires Lando's logistics bluff, Han/Chewie's physical bypass, Artoo's limited credential usage and Leia's negotiation with ordinary crew. Security puts conditioned prisoners between rescuers and extraction, giving Leia hard choices about speed and safety. Individual captives: an older Alderaanian hydroengineer, a textile worker who keeps accounts on a seam, a medic who makes others eat first—give only as many named speaking roles as needed. Find Iven as separate off-manifest old cohort. Get all forty-three out if earned through difficulty; meaningful victory need not kill captives to prove stakes. One serious ally injury and lost ship/equipment/evidence are real costs. Veyr detects the boarding because restoring captive life support requires an authenticated reference handshake. He knowingly sacrifices this transport to obtain a brief paired access window; team has to choose life support now over keeping prism perfectly isolated. His trusted field cell uses that window to seize prism's writable authority functions from a still-valid palace maintenance mirror; physical core can remain with Leia until chapter 25 or be captured there. Do not make the contact magically condition anyone. End with evacuation underway, Republic already warned, but scheduled coup forced early by threatened loss of his conditioned cadre.

### 25

Return to Coruscant and hard-cut into a coup by already conditioned palace technicians/retained Imperial personnel. Because captive clinic support and hospital dispatch share municipal interfaces established in 17/20, Veyr's mirror can replay valid medical relocation orders; people carrying weapons use actual force against unconditioned officials. These acts have bounded scope: palace security, one dispatch hub, temporary assembly wing. The whole Republic does not fall. Veyr's field cell captures physical prism during an ambush at the prearranged medical transfer, enabled by the necessary rescue handshake and existing infiltrated dispatch staff, not a random stolen bag. Darren/Sela/Leia escape with recovered non-command baseline notes and captives; Veyr gets core authority but lacks clear knowledge of how much they recovered. Luke remains outside sealed palace/returns from covering extraction, so he cannot be a convenient immediate fix. End with heroes regrouping in Orl's independent maintenance depot, prisoners sheltering in distributed civilian sites, officials held under ordinary physical coercion; conditioning begins only on a few captives and is clearly not instantaneous.

### 26

Bounded regroup/commitment, not another whole explanatory chapter. Leia refuses proposed mass identity sweeps or indiscriminate blackout of medical systems. Her alternative costs speed and political protection. Sela/Iven work with consenting former captives; their old signal becomes a way to ask for presence when speakers are dangerous. Distinct assignments: Han/Chewie physically sever relay and redirect an evacuation train; Lando coordinates independent municipal operators using Bespin industrial knowledge and contact network; Artoo attacks session synchronization but cannot interpret the revocation grammar; Threepio handles that exact interpretation; Darren volunteers for a core-side reference release after a chance to decline; Luke and Leia infiltrate the fifth station. Use all five station discoveries in planning concrete weaknesses. End: routes established and action begins before Veyr finishes renewing root authority.

### 27

Infiltration via old palace maintenance approach foreshadowed in 15/17; geographically legible network: lower service ingress → reference vault → distribution spine → fifth chamber. Conditioned personnel defend key junctions. Disconnects isolate local care systems before command relays. Irreversible choice: they can seize the throne as a new Republic authority or burn the writable root after preserving non-executable patient references. Leia chooses destruction of reusable authority; Darren explicitly agrees concerning his own data, other victims' intact records kept securely for consent-based care. Physically sever a bridge or burn a controller so reversal is no longer available. Veyr splits the party with a legitimate industrial hazard, not a convenient hologram maze. End: Luke and Leia face individualized voice assaults in distinct chambers while Darren/Threepio/Artoo reach the reference side.

### 28

Leia hears Bail; Luke a reconstruction of Anakin. Ordinary cloned archival speech is mixed with Witness Zero pattern to trigger personal experiences. No secret prior conditioning and no Veyr omniscience of untold private memories. Use source material already available (Senate archives, Vader/Temple logs) plus listeners' own associations. Luke recognizes absence of his father's genuine presence. Recognition hurts and does not make emotional longing vanish. Leia rejects an imitation's request to surrender other people's decisions in return for a perfect unlost home. Physical tasks continue during these scenes: she frees a restrained aide, Luke holds open a failing barrier. Their choosing others under pressure defeats the manipulation. Witness Zero garden returns as an offered refuge rather than an instruction: one may remember it without entering/staying/obeying. No authoritative new Force lecture. End: Leia reaches patient reference side and Luke reaches Veyr, both exhausted but capable.

### 29

Luke versus Veyr in the control gantry above reference vault. Veyr has installed himself as perpetually renewed authorizer and subjected himself to narrowing reinforcement. His certainty makes him unable to reconsider a failing defense; he drives conditioned guards into lethal positions because preservation of command has become his fixed objective. Luke spends real time and risk saving them, destroying weapon emitters and restraint feeds with competent lightsaber/Force work. He refuses the easy lethal solution that would leave active failsafes and victims trapped. Veyr's private self-conditioning mechanism is visible and documented, not just confessed. Luke disables its immediate dangerous output and captures him, without magically restoring remorse or freeing all patients. A severed automatic queue continues; practical climax remains. End: Veyr restrained, central cascade already running, heroes have a narrow engineered opportunity to stop it.

### 30

One coordinated continuous climax. Han/Chewie sever external command relay while preserving a separate evacuation/life-support circuit; Chewie holds an injured colleague's weight and gets his own engineering judgment acknowledged. Lando coordinates synchronized independent dispatch cutovers with tangible transport stakes. Artoo burns the writable mirror/session registry under fire while Threepio distinguishes historical quoted command text from active revocation grammar. Leia/medical teams call patients by chosen names and ask for observable individual responses; no entire galaxy suddenly healed by her speech. Darren physically presents his reference and authorizes its dissociation; during an involuntary command he uses one still-free action to hold/release the correct mechanical contact. His decisive contribution is a choice with accessible prior groundwork, not superhuman will that shames those still affected. Core exposed, reference evidence copied to a read-only offline store under custody, authority root and calibrated command bridge physically destroyed, all authorized cached sessions revoked/isolated. Dagobah remote path is disconnected through the recovered calibration chain; show actual confirmation or send local professionals afterward, not impossible distant explosions. Program decisively loses capacity to issue/reinforce commands. Existing conditioning and injuries persist and receive care. End on rescue/accounting and quiet acknowledgment of those still needing help, not another ominous light.

### 31

Weeks later; multiple brief but fully dramatized closure scenes. Forty-three Alderaanians named/accounted and able to choose residence, care and political involvement; no obligation to become public emblems. Darren chooses his own first journey/home, with continuing therapy and no military duty imposed. Sela and Iven reunite with Nemi/protectedcontact and recover definite truth of other two; wooden star returned without demanding Sela remain a child or join Jedi school. Earlier five-child roster preserved publicly with consent/death respect. Plagueis's droid and spindle have bounded custody history revealed through recovered provenance, then non-operational spindle returned to the cave or securely separated from instrument, with public historical findings excluding executable command recipes and personal medical records. Tell the truth about crimes without publishing victims' intimate baselines. Veyr alive in custody, evidence supports trial, no redemption cliffhanger. Lando restores damaged livelihoods/transport and accepts useful responsibility with charm. Han/Leia share warm ordinary affection and make a plan together. Luke prepares a school with an actual open-door exchange: a prospective learner asks whether they can leave; yes. Finish with earned continuation of life, no new villain teaser.
